The idea

More than splits and charts.

A running coach should remember the context: a tough week, a good workout, a niggling knee, or simply how a run felt. JogLLM aims to bring those pieces together without turning training into another full-time job.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Make sense of a run

Explore pace, heart rate, workout structure, and trends from recorded activity data.

02 / REMEMBER

Keep the human details

Put subjective effort, recovery, and notes alongside the numbers rather than losing them in a spreadsheet.

03 / PLAN

Think about what's next

Experiment with practical training recommendations and, eventually, connected structured workouts.

Under the hood

Built for curiosity, not a launch countdown.

JogLLM is being developed independently as a personal learning and running project. It is not yet a publicly available app or coaching service.

Where it stands

Initial experiments with personal Garmin data import and FIT analysis have been completed in a development environment. A dedicated Android app and optional Garmin workout publishing are future goals, subject to technical development and any required API authorization.

Independent project. JogLLM is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Garmin or OpenAI. There is currently no public sign-up or Garmin account connection on this website.
