Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Applies to the JogLLM project website and describes possible future app functionality separately.

JogLLM is an independent personal software project exploring running analytics and conversational coaching. It is currently a prototype, not a public app or service. This page explains what the current website does and the privacy practices intended for future connected features. The policy will be revised before any broader public launch or material change in data handling.

1. Data collected by this website today

This is a static informational website. It does not have user accounts, an analytics tracker, an advertising system, contact forms, or an interface for uploading running activities. We do not intentionally collect personal information through the website.

The website hosting provider may automatically process technical information, such as IP addresses, device or browser information, and request logs, to deliver and secure the pages. Its own terms and privacy practices govern that hosting activity.

2. Planned Garmin and running-data features

In a future JogLLM application, a user may optionally authorize access to their activity and health-related data from Garmin Connect or other sources. Depending on the user’s authorization and which features have actually been implemented, relevant data may include:

  • Recorded activities, workout dates, durations, distance, pace, lap and GPS route information.
  • Heart-rate and recovery measures such as resting heart rate, HRV and sleep information.
  • Training plans, workout descriptions, and subjective notes such as effort or soreness.
  • Basic account identifiers and connection credentials needed for an authorized integration.

These are proposed features, not a claim that this website currently collects or syncs those data. Any integration would require appropriate authorization, and the actual information used would depend on the scope granted and implementation.

3. Why data may be used

If connected features are developed and enabled, running data would be used to review workouts, track trends and recovery, prepare personalized coaching suggestions, and potentially create training workouts for compatible devices. The project is not intended to provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

4. Storage, AI processing, and third parties

The prototype has been explored with local scripts and private storage. A future app may use local storage, user-authorized cloud storage, and an AI service for selected coaching analysis. Before such services are enabled, the exact providers, storage locations, security practices, and data flows will be documented here. Data will not be represented as staying entirely on-device if it is sent to a cloud or AI provider.

Garmin Connect data, when accessed under authorization, would remain subject to Garmin’s applicable platform terms and the user’s Garmin permissions. JogLLM is not affiliated with Garmin or OpenAI.

5. Data sharing and sale

The current informational website does not sell personal data, run advertisements, or provide user data to advertisers. If a future feature transfers selected running information to a third-party AI or cloud provider, that transfer and its purpose will be explained before the feature is used.

6. Retention, access, and deletion

There are currently no public JogLLM accounts and no public-user training records stored through this website. Before a public app is launched, this policy will identify the retention periods, deletion methods and access requests available to users. Users of a future connected Garmin integration should be able to revoke their authorization using the applicable Garmin account or application controls.

7. Children's privacy

JogLLM is not designed or offered as a service for children. No public registration is currently available.

8. Policy changes

This privacy policy may change as the project develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date and an accurate description of the features then available.
